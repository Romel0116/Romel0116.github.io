How automatic synchronization works

  1. The owner saves the team’s Subscribe calendar URL and exact league team name.
  2. The background sync validates the TeamSideline calendar.
  3. Games and later schedule changes are imported into Firebase.
  4. Schedule changes create the same unread indicators already used on Team Home.

At the start of a new season, return here and replace the URL with the new team calendar link. TeamSideline’s calendar does not include final scores.
